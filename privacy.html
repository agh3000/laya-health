LAYA HEALTH / EARLY ACCESS

Privacy, clearly.

This notice explains how LAYA Health, an independent pre-launch project, handles information you choose to share through the LAYA Sleep early-interest form.

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Early-interest signups only. This form is not for appointments, medical advice or clinical records. Please do not submit symptoms, diagnoses, medical history or other sensitive health information.

What we collect

When you sign up, we collect the first name, email address, city, interest category, phone number with country calling code, and email contact consent you provide. A phone number is now required for joining the list, but permission to be contacted by call or WhatsApp remains optional and separate. If you indicate that you are a potential customer, you may optionally select an indicative price range for an overnight sleep assessment. Our form service may process technical information necessary to deliver the form and limit spam.

Why we collect it

We use your details to register your interest, email you about LAYA Sleep launch plans or possible pilot participation, and follow up about a partnership or investment inquiry if relevant. We collect a phone number as part of your early-interest contact details, but we will not initiate promotional calls or WhatsApp messages unless you separately opt in. Optional expected-price responses are used in aggregate to research demand and pricing; they are not a booking commitment, medical quotation or promised product price. Signing up does not guarantee early access, appointments or eligibility for a clinical service.

Who receives the information

The form is processed by FormSubmit, a third-party form-delivery service. Submissions are routed to the LAYA Health project contact inbox. We do not publish signup details or sell the information you submit. FormSubmit and the email provider may process information under their own privacy practices.

Retention and choices

We keep contact details for pre-launch communications until they are no longer needed or you ask us to delete them. You can ask to view, correct or delete your signup details, or stop further messages, by emailing agupta2@g.harvard.edu with “LAYA Sleep privacy request” in the subject. Because the venture is pre-launch, this address is a founder contact, not a clinical support channel.

Important boundaries

LAYA Health is not currently offering medical services. No affiliated university, hospital, clinician, wearable brand or partner is endorsing or operating the signup form. This notice may be updated as our services and data practices develop.
