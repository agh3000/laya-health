WHY LAYA EXISTS

The missing health check.

We regularly check our blood. But most of us have never had a meaningful assessment of what happens to our brain, breathing and body while we sleep.

My research focuses on analyzing overnight sleep studies with AI—finding patterns in complex physiological signals and understanding how clinical labels are interpreted. It showed me both the power of these measurements and how far the experience is from everyday preventive health.

That's why I'm building LAYA Health. We are starting with LAYA Sleep: a vision for making clinical-grade sleep assessments more accessible, welcoming and actionable, with follow-up monitoring and appropriate care beyond a single night.
